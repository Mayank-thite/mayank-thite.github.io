50% of withdrawals happen before the course starts or in its first four weeks

Before you act on this

  • The A/B is observational. Students picked their own group, so motivation I can't measure could explain all of the gap. A randomised pre-start email would answer it.
  • The early-warning model is weak. At day 28 most later withdrawals still look like everyone else, so I'd use it for a short advisor call list, not a verdict on anyone.
  • 2013 to 2014 data from one UK distance-learning university. Modules are anonymised.
