Caveats

  • 99.44% of timestamps are exactly midnight, so I only know the date. A headline dated t may have come out before the open or after the close. That is why "t and t+1" is shown too.
  • The same day link does not mean news moves prices. Many headlines describe a move that already happened.
  • Fama-MacBeth daily regressions give a mean news day slope of 0.264 pp of return per unit of sentiment, Newey-West t = 6.44, over 1,249 dates.
  • Per ticker, 2 of 77 tickers have a next day p below 0.05. Chance alone gives about 4. After a Holm correction, 0 remain.
  • Swapping VADER for the Loughran-McDonald finance word lists gives the same shape: r = 0.040 on the news day (t = 8.15) and 0.006 the day after (t = 1.19), both net of the market.
  • Not a trading signal. No costs, no out of sample test.
