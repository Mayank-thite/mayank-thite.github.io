Of the 828 A SKUs, 106 are smooth, 432 erratic, 207 lumpy and 83 intermittent (Syntetos Boylan Croston rules on weekly demand). The normal formula gave a lower reorder point than the empirical one for 647 of them. There are no stock levels in the data, so these are where a planner would reorder, not current positions. The 2 week lead time is my assumption.

Champions are 23.6% of these customers and 69.3% of their net revenue, which is 59.9% of all net revenue. 13.6% of net revenue comes from sales with no customer ID, so it is in the totals above but not here.

Caveats

Cancellations are not the same as returns. Some are order corrections. Two bulk orders of 74,215 and 80,995 units were cancelled the same day; they net to zero in the weekly demand, so they do not inflate velocity or ABC.

Revenue is list price times quantity. There is no cost data, so there is no margin.

The reorder points come from one year of history and overlapping 2 week windows, so the 95th percentile rests on a handful of big weeks per SKU. Treat them as a starting point.
